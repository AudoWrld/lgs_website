About LGS

Our Analytical Approach

Structured, Traceable, Reliable

Our analytical workflow integrates controlled sample preparation, wet-chemistry techniques and Atomic Absorption Spectrometry (AAS) for quantitative elemental determination.

From sample receipt through preparation, analysis, quality control and final reporting, samples are managed through structured laboratory procedures designed to maintain sample identity, analytical consistency and traceability.

Our Services

Gold, Silver, Copper and Sulphur analysis, alongside specialized metallurgical and technical testing services. Click any service to read more.

Elemental Analysis

Gold (Au) Analysis

Quantitative determination of gold in geological and mineral-processing samples using controlled chemical preparation, solvent extraction and Atomic Absorption Spectrometry (AAS). Our gold analytical services support exploration, grade evaluation and process-monitoring applications.

Silver (Ag) Analysis

Quantitative determination of silver in geological and process samples, providing analytical data for mineral exploration, ore characterization and mineral-processing applications.

Copper (Cu) Analysis

Determination of copper concentrations in rocks, soils, pulps, ores, tailings and other mineral-related materials using instrumental analytical techniques.

Sulphur Analysis

Determination of sulphur content in mineral samples to support ore characterization, metallurgical assessment and mineral-processing evaluation.

Metallurgical & Process Testing

Carbon Activity Testing

Evaluation of activated carbon adsorption performance for gold recovery applications. Carbon activity testing provides useful information for assessing carbon performance and supporting the management of adsorption circuits.

Cyanide Leaching Tests

Controlled laboratory-scale cyanide leaching tests for evaluating the gold extraction characteristics and potential metallurgical response of gold-bearing materials.

Cyanide Leaching Parameter Optimization

Systematic evaluation of key process parameters influencing cyanide leaching performance. Testing can assist in evaluating operating conditions for improved gold extraction and process efficiency.

Metallic Screening & Gold Evaluation

Specialized evaluation of heterogeneous or coarse-gold-bearing samples where conventional sample preparation may be affected by uneven gold distribution or the nugget effect.

Technical Assistance & Consultancy

Technical assistance in areas related to mineral analysis, sample preparation, analytical quality control and selected mineral-processing applications. We work with clients to understand their analytical requirements and provide appropriate laboratory testing solutions.

LGS provides mineral analytical and metallurgical testing services on a quotation basis. Share your sample and service requirements and our team will review your request and provide guidance on the appropriate service and quotation process.

Why Quote-Based Pricing?

Sample type, quantity, preparation requirements and the specific analytical service requested can all affect turnaround and testing requirements. Rather than publish fixed prices that may not reflect your actual needs, LGS reviews each request individually to provide accurate, relevant pricing and technical guidance.

LGS provides mineral analytical, metallurgical testing, laboratory supply and CRM services on a quotation basis. Select the services and methods you need below and our team will review your request and provide guidance and a quotation.

Why Quote-Based Pricing?

Sample type, quantity, preparation requirements and the specific services requested can all affect turnaround and testing requirements. Rather than publish fixed prices that may not reflect your actual needs, LGS reviews each request individually to provide accurate, relevant pricing and technical guidance.

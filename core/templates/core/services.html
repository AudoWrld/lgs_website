What We Offer

Mineral Assay & Metallurgical Testing Services

LGS provides mineral analysis, metallurgical testing, certified reference materials, laboratory supplies and equipment sourcing, and technical consultancy for exploration, mining and mineral-processing clients.

01

Mineral Analysis

Quantitative determination of key elements in geological and process samples using controlled chemical preparation and instrumental analytical techniques.

01

Gold & Copper Analysis

Combined quantitative determination of gold and copper in geological and mineral-processing samples, supporting exploration, grade evaluation and process-monitoring applications.

02

Gold, Copper & Silver Analysis

Combined quantitative determination of gold, copper and silver in geological and process samples, providing analytical data for exploration and ore characterization.

03

Gold, Copper & Sulphur Analysis

Combined determination of gold, copper and sulphur content to support ore characterization, metallurgical assessment and mineral-processing evaluation.

04

Gold, Copper, Silver & Sulphur Analysis

Combined quantitative determination of gold, copper, silver and sulphur in geological and process samples, supporting ore characterization and metallurgical assessment.

05

Multi-Element Analysis

Determination of multiple elements in a single sample for a broader analytical profile, supporting exploration and process-monitoring decisions.

02

Metallurgical Testing

Specialized testing for cyanide leaching performance and heterogeneous gold-bearing samples.

06

Conventional Cyanide Leaching Test

Controlled laboratory-scale cyanide leaching tests for evaluating the gold extraction characteristics and potential metallurgical response of gold-bearing materials.

07

Cyanide Leaching Parameter Optimization

Systematic evaluation of key process parameters influencing cyanide leaching performance, assisting in evaluating operating conditions for improved gold extraction and process efficiency.

08

Carbon Activity Test

Evaluation of activated carbon performance to support carbon-in-pulp and carbon-in-leach process monitoring and recovery assessment.

09

Metallic Screening & Gold Evaluation

Specialized evaluation of heterogeneous or coarse-gold-bearing samples where conventional sample preparation may be affected by uneven gold distribution or the nugget effect.

03

Certified Reference Materials (CRM)

High-quality Certified Reference Materials for use in mineral assay laboratories as part of routine Quality Assurance and Quality Control (QA/QC) programs.

10

Rock Pulp Certified Reference Materials

CRMs prepared from rock pulp for use in laboratory QA/QC programs, supporting analytical accuracy and consistency across testing runs.

11

Gold-Loaded Carbon Certified Reference Materials

CRMs prepared from gold-loaded carbon for use in laboratory QA/QC programs related to carbon-in-leach and carbon-in-pulp processes.

04

Laboratory Supplies & Equipment

Sourcing and supply solutions for laboratory equipment, analytical instruments, glassware, consumables, tools, accessories and sample preparation equipment for mineral, metallurgical and analytical laboratories. Clients may submit required items, quantities, technical specifications and preferred brands or models, and our team will assist with sourcing and supply.

12

Laboratory Glassware & Consumables

Sourcing and supply of laboratory glassware and general consumables for mineral, metallurgical and analytical laboratory use.

13

Laboratory Equipment & Instruments

Sourcing and supply of laboratory equipment and analytical instruments based on client-specified technical requirements and preferred brands or models.

14

Laboratory Tools & Accessories

Sourcing and supply of general laboratory tools and accessories to support day-to-day laboratory operations.

15

Spectrophotometers & Analytical Instruments

Sourcing and supply of spectrophotometers and other analytical instruments for mineral and metallurgical laboratory applications.

05

Technical Services

Technical support beyond routine laboratory analysis, tailored to client requirements.

16

Technical Assistance & Consultancy

Technical assistance in areas related to mineral analysis, sample preparation, analytical quality control and selected mineral-processing applications. LGS works with clients to understand their analytical requirements and provide appropriate laboratory testing solutions.

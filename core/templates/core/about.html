About LGS

A Mineral Analytical Laboratory Built on Trust

LGS Mineral Assay Laboratory is a mineral analytical and testing laboratory providing analytical and technical services to the mining, mineral exploration and mineral processing industries.

Who We Are

Supporting Every Stage of the Mineral Value Chain

LGS Mineral Assay Laboratory is a mineral analytical and testing laboratory providing analytical and technical services to the mining, mineral exploration and mineral processing industries. The laboratory supports exploration programs, ore evaluation, metallurgical investigations and mineral-processing operations.

Our Laboratory Approach

Controlled, Consistent, Traceable

The laboratory uses controlled sample preparation, wet-chemistry techniques and Atomic Absorption Spectrometry (AAS) for quantitative elemental determination. Sample management extends from receipt through preparation, analysis, quality control and final reporting.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognized mineral analytical laboratory in Tanzania and across Africa, delivering reliable analytical data that supports responsible mineral exploration, mining and mineral-processing development.

Our Mission

To provide accurate, reliable and timely mineral analytical and metallurgical testing services through controlled laboratory procedures, appropriate analytical technology, effective quality assurance and competent technical personnel, enabling our clients to make informed technical and operational decisions.

Our Motto
The Lab You Can Trust

Our motto represents our commitment to reliability, integrity, consistency and confidence in analytical results.
